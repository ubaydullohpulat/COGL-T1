### Summary
Built SwarmLab v0, a Monte Carlo emulator of Ling-3.0-flash split across 2–8 home peers, and swept 3 topologies × 6 link profiles.
Over home internet, network time is ~95% of a token, so throughput is set by how many sequential network crossings a token needs.
A pipeline across a few peers gives ~2–4 tok/s at 137 ms. Naively sharding experts across peers (a round trip in every MoE layer)
gives ~0.12 tok/s, 20–30× worse, even though its per-peer memory is lowest. That makes the project's core bet conditional:
expert-level sharding works only if routing locality lets most layers avoid a remote round trip. Real routing traces must now answer that.
Prefill is bandwidth-bound: ~9 s for a 1k-token prompt over a 4-peer chain at bf16, ~5 s at int8.

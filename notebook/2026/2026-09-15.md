## Session 2

**Phase / test:** Phase 0 / T0 — step 1.1 (pin the target)
**Session goal (agreed):** Adopt plan amendments, init git, execute T0 step 1.1. Researcher's direction: final product is Kimi K3; all testing on this Mac using the smaller same-family model Kimi Linear, simulating multiple peers on one machine.

### Summary
Both models are pinned to exact revisions with their configs, code, license and tokenizer saved and hashed.
Three things changed our picture. First, K3 is much deeper than assumed: 93 layers, so naive
one-round-trip-per-layer decode is ~0.07 tok/s at 150 ms, below the T0 kill line. The topology choice in 1.3 decides the gate.
Second, K3's AttnRes makes the full inter-layer state up to 9× hidden size, while its LatentMoE shrinks an expert call's
payload to half a hidden vector. Both push toward "layer host fans out to expert peers" rather than peer chains.
Third, Kimi Linear is K3's own model code with LatentMoE and AttnRes switched off: a good proxy for attention, router and
tokenizer, but not for expert granularity or those two features.
